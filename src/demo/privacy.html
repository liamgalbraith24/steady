Steady privacy policy

Short version: Steady does not collect, sell, or send any of your data. Everything stays on your computer.

What Steady stores

  • Your settings (profiles, site lists) are saved with Chrome's own sync storage (chrome.storage.sync). If you are signed in to Chrome with sync turned on, Chrome copies them to your other computers. Steady has no server.
  • Research study log (off by default). If you turn on "Record a study log", Steady saves interaction events, such as "a dwell click happened" or "a target was highlighted", on your computer only (chrome.storage.local). It never includes page text, form contents, passwords, or the addresses of pages you visit. You can export or delete the log from the Steady toolbar button at any time.

What Steady reads

To work, Steady runs on the pages you visit. It looks at the position of buttons and links so it can highlight them, and it reads the text of short pop-up messages so it can keep them in its "Page messages" panel. This happens inside your browser. Nothing is sent anywhere, and messages are forgotten when you close the tab.

What Steady does not do

  • No analytics, telemetry, advertising, or tracking of any kind.
  • No network requests to Steady's author or anyone else.
  • No camera or microphone access.
  • Websites cannot read your Steady settings. (The demo page can pause Steady or choose a profile for that tab only, for research tasks.)

The demo page

The demo and study page saves your task results in your browser (localStorage) until you download or delete them. It sends nothing anywhere.
